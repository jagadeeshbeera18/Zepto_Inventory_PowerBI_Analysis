The stock view deduplicates rows only when product, pack, listed prices, discount and availability all match across category tags. Category charts show category listings and can therefore overlap. Available quantity is treated as a snapshot count; value uses quantity × listed selling price. No sales velocity, lead time or reorder policy is included, so low quantity is a review flag rather than an automatic replenishment recommendation.
